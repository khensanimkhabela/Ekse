# Fimiya — Ekse, Shine Yakithi.

**The AI-powered creative economy platform for South African artists.**

## The Problem

South Africa's creative economy is informal, fragmented, and unprotected:

- **97%** of local artists struggle to find exposure or gigs
- **67%** report not getting paid after performing
- **80%** have no copyright protection on their work
- **99%** sit outside the formal tax system

Organizers face the mirror problem: no reliable way to find and vet talent on short notice, and no accountability layer when a booking goes wrong.

## The Solution

Fimiya unifies discovery, contracts, payment, and growth into one platform:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Smart Artist Discovery** | AI matching by genre, location, budget & availability — multilingual (English, isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho) |
| **AI Contract Generator + Risk Scoring** | Fair, legally grounded contracts in seconds, benchmarked against market rates |
| **Escrow Payments** | Artists paid within 48 hours of a gig, held safely until milestone confirmation |
| **AI Tax Assistant** | SARS-aware provisional tax estimates, auto-categorized expenses, filing reminders |
| **AI Visibility Engine** | Auto-generated bios, captions and posters, tuned per platform |

## Business Model

- **Booking commissions:** 10–20%
- **Ticketing fees:** 5–10%
- **Subscriptions:** $10–100/month (Free / Pro / Agency)
- **Event add-ons:** $5–50
- **Financial services:** 1–3% payout fee

## Why Fimiya Wins

> "Artists don't need five tools — they need one platform that understands them."

No competitor combines all five: Ticketpro and Eventbrite handle ticketing only; Upwork has no local rails or SA-specific tools; Patreon and Bandcamp don't touch discovery, contracts, or tax. Fimiya is the only platform with **artist discovery + AI contracts + escrow + SA tax tools + AI visibility + local language support + PayFast/Ozow rails**, all in one flow:

🔍 Discover → 📄 Contract → 💰 Get Paid → 🧾 Stay Compliant → 🎨 Get Seen — *one flow, one platform, zero gaps.*
